Sector Performance Lab

Which industries actually rewarded investors from 1990 to 2026 — and at what risk? A from-scratch study of 37 sector portfolios across the US and India: returns, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Treynor, alpha, drawdowns, correlations, and an honest test of whether past winners keep winning.
